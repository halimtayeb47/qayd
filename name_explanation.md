**App Name:** Qayd (قَيْد)

**1. Cultural & Academic Depth**
The name is inspired by the famous Arabic scholarly proverb: *"Knowledge is a hunted prey, and writing is its tether (Qayd)."* 
A lecture is full of fleeting information (the prey). The app acts as the **"Qayd" (tether/binding)** that captures the professor's spoken words, transcribes them, and secures them into a structured summary so they are never lost.

**2. HCI & UX Perspective**
* **Mental Model (Affordance):** The word implies "securing, locking, and saving." It instantly tells the user that this app is a safe vault for fast-paced lectures.
* **Memorability:** It is a punchy, one-syllable, 3-letter word. This makes it highly memorable, easy to type, and perfect for modern mobile app branding.
* **Emotional Design (Anxiety Reduction):** Students often feel anxious about missing key points during a lecture. The name "Qayd" provides a psychological sense of security, assuring them that every word is safely anchored. 

**3. Seamless UI Integration**
The name naturally translates into app terminology. Instead of standard buttons like "Record" or "Saved Files," the interface can use tailored, intuitive microcopy like *"Start Qayd (Binding)"* or *"Your Qayds (Records),"* making the user experience highly cohesive.
