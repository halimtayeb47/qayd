Cell Biology — Membranes

Today · 48 min · Mixed

Overview

Cell membranes regulate what enters and leaves the cell. Their selective permeability depends on the phospholipid bilayer and embedded proteins.

Key points

  • The phospholipid bilayer forms the membrane’s basic structure.
  • Transport proteins help specific molecules cross the membrane.
  • Membrane fluidity changes with temperature and lipid composition.
